Mini Delivery — Quick Guide

Package S (Circle)

Light parcel. Flown across water by Cargo Drones (1×) or carried on lines by Cyberbikes (2×) & Cybercars (4×).

Package L (Square)

Appears mid-Week 1. Too heavy for drones! Requires a line with a Cyberbike (1×) or Cybercar (2×).

Package XL (Diamond)

Heavy freight from Week 2! Can only be carried by a Cybercar (1×) along ground lines.

• Draw Lines & Connector Plugs: Drag from station to station. Grab the T-terminals or the connector plugs on any station to unplug and re-dock lines elsewhere.

• Dock Drag & Drop: Drag Bikes, Cars, Drones, and S/L/XL Hubs directly from the bottom dock onto lines, depots, or the map!

• Auto-Zoom Camera: The camera starts close in the city center and smoothly zooms out as your metropolis expands.
